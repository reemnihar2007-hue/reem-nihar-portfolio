PROJECT 02

Organisational Behaviour & Management

An individual business case essay examining how Mastercard's strategy of enabling SMEs to access credit and financial services could affect the organisation and its main business functions.

What I Did

I analysed Scenario 3, in which Mastercard planned to roll out its Enabling SMEs initiative to ensure that micro, small and medium-sized enterprises could access credit and other financial services. I examined how this strategic change could affect Marketing, Human Resources, Operations Management, Information Systems and Finance.

What I Learned

This project developed my understanding of organisational behaviour, strategic change and the relationship between different business functions. It also strengthened my ability to apply module concepts, develop a critical discussion and support an academic business case analysis with wider reading.

Organisational Behaviour Strategic Change Business Analysis Critical Thinking
